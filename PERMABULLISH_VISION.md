# Permabullish - Vision Document

## The Name
**Permabullish** — A play on "perma-bull" (permanently bullish), reflecting optimism and long-term wealth creation mindset.

---

## What is Permabullish?

Permabullish is an **AI-powered investment research and advisory platform** designed for Indian investors. It provides comprehensive tools to analyze, compare, and make informed decisions across all major equity investment vehicles.

---

## The Suite

| Module | Status | Description |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| **Stock Research Generator** | MVP Complete | AI-powered fundamental & technical analysis of individual stocks |
| **PMS Tracker** | MVP Complete | Analytics and comparison of 219+ Portfolio Management Services |
| **Mutual Fund Analytics** | Planned | Fund comparison, SIP planning, category analysis |
| **Portfolio Builder** | Planned | Asset allocation recommendations based on risk profile |
| **AI Advisor** | Planned | Personalized investment recommendations and chat interface |

---

## Core Value Proposition

> "Should I pick stocks myself, hire a PMS, or invest through mutual funds — and which ones?"

Permabullish answers this fundamental investor question with data, analysis, and AI-powered insights.

---

## Target Users

1. **Retail Investors** — Mutual funds, select stocks
2. **HNIs/UHNIs** — PMS evaluation, direct equity, portfolio construction
3. **DIY Investors** — Stock research, self-managed portfolios
4. **Financial Advisors** — Client research, product comparison

---

## Data Sources

- **SEBI** — PMS disclosures, regulatory filings
- **AMFI** — Mutual fund NAVs, AUM, portfolios
- **BSE/NSE** — Stock prices, fundamentals, corporate actions
- **AI Models** — Analysis, insights, recommendations
